ABOUT ME

Yutong Wu — Ella

CS + Informatics @ UCI

I believe that the emotional resonance and sense of calm we find in many things—from art and film to games—stem from the unique atmosphere they create. Architecture, sensory experiences, and music or art all strive to achieve this feeling of immersion and presence, and technology is accelerating the creation of such experiences. Therefore, my interest is dedicated to exploring the origins of these atmospheres, how they can be recreated, and how we achieve deep immersion.
